My paper *AIDAL-ARG at Daleel 2026: Hybrid and Multitask Approaches for Arabic
Argument Mining* (joint work with Karla-Salas Jimenez and Leila Moudjari) has been accepted for publication in the [Arabic Argumentative Discourse Mining Shared Task](https://qatardebate.org/programs/academic-programs/daleel2026-shared-task/) (Daleel 2026) where it was awarded of the second prize of the competition, and will be presented during the ArabicNLP/EMNLP conference.
